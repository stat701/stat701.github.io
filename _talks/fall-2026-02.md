title: "Bayesian Multi-stream Change-point Detection"
---

In high-dimensional monitoring applications, changes often affect only a small and unknown subset of data streams. We develop a Bayesian framework for joint changepoint detection and localization that combines likelihood evidence across possible changepoints and affected subsets, incorporating structured priors to represent plausible change patterns. Our approach provides false-alarm guarantees and posterior inference on when and where changes occur. We establish theoretical results characterizing the benefits of pooling information across affected streams and the influence of prior specification on detection delay. We illustrate the methodology through numerical experiments with independent and spatially dependent observations, motivated by fault detection in CERN's electromagnetic calorimeter.
