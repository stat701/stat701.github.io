title: "High-dimensional image recovery for real-time radiological mapping"
---

Radiological mapping aims to reconstruct spatial radiation activity from noisy measurements collected by moving detectors, such as drones. However, limited measurement budgets and increasingly high-resolution maps make accurate reconstruction and uncertainty quantification computationally challenging. In this talk, we propose BayeSTAR (Bayesian Structure-Aware Radiological mapping), a framework that combines low-rank matrix modeling, hierarchical shrinkage, and spatial smoothing to capture important features of radiation activity maps. The model is fitted using low-rank variational inference (VI), enabling efficient estimation and uncertainty quantification. Through synthetic experiments, we demonstrate that our approach achieves improved reconstruction accuracy and competitive probabilistic predictions compared with established benchmark methods, while showing promise for scaling to higher-resolution maps.
