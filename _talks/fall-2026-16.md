title: "Learning Reliable Prediction Sets Under Distribution Shift"
---

Prediction sets are useful only when their uncertainty guarantees survive contact with data that differ from the training sample. This talk introduces conformal prediction and asks how its coverage behavior changes under covariate shift. I will begin with the intuition behind distribution-free marginal coverage and use simple examples to show why ordinary conformal methods can fail for a shifted target population. I will then describe a research-in-progress approach that combines weighted conformity scores with diagnostics for unstable importance weights. The emphasis will be on the statistical question, the main ideas behind the proposed method, and what the remaining limitations teach us about uncertainty quantification—not on a dense catalog of technical results. The goal is to give the audience a clear entry point to conformal prediction under shift and a useful framework for thinking about when its guarantees are scientifically meaningful.
