01 / The seminar

A forum for statistical ideas, clearly explained.

A strong presentation makes an interesting idea accessible.

STA 701S is a working forum for learning across areas and practicing the clear communication of technical ideas. Presenters choose a topic worth understanding, build a thoughtful and engaging talk, and create a conversation that helps speaker and audience think more carefully.

I

Choose

Select an interesting statistical idea, establish why it matters, and make it accessible without sacrificing depth.

II

Explain

Build a focused, accurate, and engaging presentation that gives the audience a clear path into the idea.

III

Discuss

Use constructive questions to test understanding, surface assumptions, and connect ideas across statistical science.

02 / Participation

Every talk is a shared responsibility.

Speakers bring an idea worth understanding; the room helps make sense of it.

  1. 01

    Presenters prepare a focused, high-quality talk that gives the audience enough context to engage deeply.

  2. 02

    The topic may come from the presenter's own research, but it need not. The central task is to explain an interesting statistical idea with clarity, depth, and care.

  3. 03

    Participants listen actively, ask constructive questions, and help the room reach a deeper understanding.
