01 / The seminar

A forum for research as it develops.

Research improves when it is explained to others.

STA 701S is a working forum for sharing ideas in development, learning across areas, and practicing the clear communication of technical work. The goal is not only to report results, but to create a conversation that helps both speaker and audience think more carefully.

I

Share

Frame a research problem, show what is new, and make open questions visible.

II

Question

Use discussion and constructive feedback to test assumptions and sharpen the story.

III

Connect

Encounter the breadth of work across the department and learn about research areas and potential advisors.

02 / Participation

Every talk is a shared responsibility.

Speakers bring the research; the room helps sharpen it.

  1. 01

    Presenters give the audience enough context to engage, then leave room for questions and discussion.

  2. 02

    Participants listen actively, ask constructive questions, and offer feedback that helps the work move forward.

  3. 03

    Current enrollment and presentation expectations live in Duke's official Ph.D. program requirements.
